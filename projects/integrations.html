The work between the systems.

Business software rarely lives alone. An order connects commerce, customer records, invoicing, inventory, and the people fulfilling it. When those records drift apart, people end up doing the integration manually.

At Super-Sod, my work spans applications and the services connecting them: order ingestion, data reconciliation, CRM synchronization, and operational workflows.

Make discrepancies visible, then handle them.

I improved Shopify-to-ERP order processing by addressing webhook ingestion failures and data inconsistencies. I also implemented an AI-assisted reconciliation workflow that compares Shopify and ERP orders, corrects discrepancies, and records its actions.

Remaining exceptions go to staff for review. That review boundary matters: automation is most useful when its actions are visible and the cases it cannot resolve have somewhere to go.

My contribution: order-processing improvements and AI-assisted reconciliation, alongside the applications and database workflows those processes depend on.

Connect the customer record.

I built and maintained ERP-to-HubSpot synchronization covering deals, contacts, companies, associations, and line items. The work connects operational data to CRM records so teams can use the information in the context they need.

It combines API integration, relational data, and business rules rather than treating synchronization as a simple copy operation.

Backend work with business context.

I develop Go REST APIs and PostgreSQL functions, triggers, views, and integrity rules supporting operational and analytical data flows. On the application side, I build and maintain SvelteKit and TypeScript functionality for ordering, invoicing, inventory, pricing, dispatch, and purchasing.

Working across those layers helps me connect a user-facing problem to the data and service behavior underneath it.

The work continues after the request.

I implement background-processing components with Python/Flask, Celery, RabbitMQ, and Docker, and support Azure deployments and GitHub Actions workflows.

I work with teammates to extend existing services, troubleshoot production behavior across systems, and maintain the workflows that take changes into production.

What I take from this work.

A good integration reduces repetitive effort while making operations easier to understand. I care about the underlying problem, clear failure behavior, and software that stays maintainable as the business changes.
