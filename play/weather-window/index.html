Turn up the rain. Follow the clouds. Move from bright daylight to the blue of a late night. This is an illustrated scene you control, rather than a live weather report.

How the scene works

Layers of procedural scenery, seeded rain particles, and smoothly changing parameters create depth without downloading video or textures. Particle counts are bounded. Motion pauses when the scene is offscreen or the tab is hidden; reduced-motion settings start with a still scene.
