Draw a continuous line with your mouse, pen, or finger. Try a new seed to vary the same strokes, or switch modes to see them differently. Undo restores earlier changes, including a cleared canvas. The examples and settings work with a keyboard too.

How the sketchbook works

Normalized strokes and a seed define each piece. Botanical mode grows bounded branches and leaves; Flow arranges a bounded particle field around the same gestures. Undo rebuilds the piece from its drawing data. A dedicated export render creates the 1800 × 1200 PNG.

Drafts are saved only when you choose Save local draft, on this browser. Drawings are not uploaded. The sketchbook limits strokes, points, generated shapes, and undo history to keep work and memory bounded.
